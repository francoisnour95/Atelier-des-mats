Your beams

One line per log: length, then diameter. Put 4x in front for several identical logs, and a name before a colon if you like.

Examples: 3 m 10 cm · 4x 250 cm 8 cm · Mât: 6 m 15 cm. Diameter defaults to 10 cm.

    My ID

    Save your scenes online and find them on your phone, tablet or computer. No email: just a nickname and a PIN.

    Pick a nickname, not your real name. Write your PIN down: without an email it cannot be reset, but everything stays on this device anyway.

    Signed in as

    Your scenes are kept on this device and online. Sign in with the same nickname and PIN on another device to find them there.

    Install the app

    1. Open this page in Safari.
    2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
    3. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add”.

    The app gets its own icon and works without internet. It keeps its own copy of your scenes, so sign in with your ID to find them there.
